1 · Align by hand

The overlay is an ideal equiangular fisheye (180° across the disc) for this time and place, north up. Rotate, mirror and drag the photo until the real stars sit on the circles: bigger circles are brighter stars, the faint guide rings mark 30°, 60° and 90° from the zenith, the dashed circle is the disc rim and the cross its centre (the zenith). Drag to shift · arrow keys nudge 1 px, shift+arrow 10 px.

Pin a star instead of dragging: click its circle, then the same star in the photo. Every pin is fitted at once, so the lines show the error each pair still carries. Four stars also calibrate the lens — the circles then move onto the real stars, and a fifth settles the theta^5 term. The fit stops there: further stars tighten it instead of buying more distortion terms. Click a pinned circle again — or the ✕ beside it in the readout — to take that pin out.
